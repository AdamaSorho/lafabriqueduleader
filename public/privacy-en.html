Privacy and Personal Data

The information collected through the forms (name, email address, WhatsApp phone, city/country, request type, desired quantity, and optional message) is used exclusively to process the services requested from La Fabrique du Leader. It is not sent to Google Analytics. It may be processed by technical providers that are strictly necessary to submit the form or send the email.

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data. To exercise your rights, please contact us at: contact@zonzerigueleadership.com.

Transactional Emails

Emails sent through the site are strictly transactional (excerpts, orders, group requests). No marketing communication is carried out without the explicit consent of the user. The data collected in this context is used solely for processing the request and in accordance with GDPR provisions.

Cookies

With your explicit consent, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure viewed pages and selected actions: navigation to offers, WhatsApp or Amazon order clicks, excerpt form submissions, and “Beyond the Book” request submissions. Parameters may identify the click source, order type, quantity, or request type. No personal contact details or free-text form content is sent to Google Analytics.

The Google Analytics tag is not loaded before you consent. Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain disabled. You can reject audience measurement or change your choice at any time through “Cookie preferences” in the footer. For more information about Google’s processing, read the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For any question related to this policy or your personal data, contact us at contact@zonzerigueleadership.com.
